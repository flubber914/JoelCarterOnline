Sir Rad

A game made in C++ with Custom engine using SDL2 for university project, and that's my voice too.

Play as Sir Rad, a knight who, instead of defending his castle from attack, uses it as an opportunity for some sick stunts!

Click here to look at the Sir Rad source code.

!Important! Press escape if your mouse is stuck, the game doesn't automatically close in browser.

To learn the controls, listen to my awful singing, the aim of this project was to combine music and tie it directly to the game mechanics.

!WARNING! The song will start and it may be loud.

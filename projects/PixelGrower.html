Pixel Grower

a game made in 2 weeks.

Play as a farmer on a floating island, buy seeds from the market to grow flowers, once they are grown, collect the gold by clicking the flower and buy more seeds. Upload an image so it shows within the upload window in game, the flowers will grow like pixels forming an image.

Click here to be brought to my Itch.io page if you wish to download it or if the player isn't working.

!Important! Playing in browser please use the full screen at the bottom by scolling on the side of the unity window, to exit press escape.

The controls and goals are on the Itch.io page

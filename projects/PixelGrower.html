Pixel Grower

2-Week Rapid Prototype • Unity & C#

Play as a farmer on a floating island, buy seeds from the market to grow flowers, once they are grown, collect the gold by clicking the flower and buy more seeds. Upload an image so it shows within the upload window in game, the flowers will grow like pixels forming an image.

Controls

  • W A S D — Move character
  • Hold RMB — Free look camera
  • RMB + LMB — Throw / Plant seed
  • E — Buy seed (at stand)
  • Space — Jump
  • LMB — Harvest fully grown flowers
  • Q — Toggle Free-Cam mode (Ascend: Space | Descend: LCtrl)

Gameplay Loop

  1. Start with 50 Gold in your inventory.
  2. Upload any custom image via the in-game UI.
  3. Purchase seeds and sow them across the island.
  4. Wait for growth cycles to complete, then harvest for profit.

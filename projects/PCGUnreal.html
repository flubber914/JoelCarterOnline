Procedural Content Generation

A mushroom cave made with procedural generation, using Houdini and Unreal Engine.

This world was made at university in my final year.

Some assets were gathered from fab.com.

The mushrooms were created within Houdini, a procedural 3D model generating program, and imported into Unreal to make random caves. The walls were also randomly enerated to create different shapes each time. The ground is painted with random mushrooms as well based on the different textures sitting on the ground and the height of the stones.

All the gampley elements within this including the world and interactions were created by me.

Gobble Goblin

A game jam game made in a day.

Play as a poisoned goblin eating fruits to survive, other goblins attack so you must switch between puzzle and shooter.

Click here to be brought to my Itch.io page if you wish to download it or if the player isn't working.

!Important! Playing in browser please use the full screen at the bottom by scolling on the side of the unity window, to exit press escape.

Press Spacebar to switch between views, fruits expand once selected to form a chain.

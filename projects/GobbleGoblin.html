Gobble Goblin

24-Hour Game Jam • Unity & C#

Play as a poisoned goblin eating fruits to survive, other goblins attack so you must switch between puzzle and shooter.

Engineered the complete gameplay pipeline from scratch. I programmed the procedural grid generation for dynamic fruit spawning, designed adjacency and colour matching algorithms to detect valid player moves, and implemented the combo-chaining logic that drives the scoring and health regeneration systems.

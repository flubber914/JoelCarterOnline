Under Iron Water

My second shipped title on Steam, proving the ability to execute a full production lifecycle from initial concept to final release.

Under Iron Water is a first-person adventure game developed entirely from the ground up in Unity. Serving as the sole programmer and designer, I architected the core gameplay loops, custom physics interactions, and enemy AI, managing the entire production pipeline through to a public launch. The project required balancing scalable, performant systems with responsive game feel, specifically focusing on first-person puzzle platforming and event-driven narrative integration

Core Contributions
  • Centralized First-Person Interaction: Architected a raycast system utilizing calls to safely handle both combat logic and puzzle triggers, combined with dynamic spatial calculations for VFX scaling.
  • Dynamic Event-Driven Dialogue: Engineered a custom narrative delivery coroutine featuring procedural pacing based on string length. Optimized execution using cached references and early-exit guard clauses to prevent garbage collection spikes.
  • World Scale & NavMesh Optimization: Constructed multi-elevation open environments. Managed the memory overhead of massive asset counts to ensure stable framerates and reliable AI pathfinding across first-person topography.
